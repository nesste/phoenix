## Accepted limitations

- Linux evidence is Ubuntu 24.04.4 under WSL2 with `CGO_ENABLED=0`, not bare metal. Benchmark production on its actual target host.
- Runtime standing tokens were measured on the constant six-verb spike schema. Flatness at 10, 100, and 1,000 verbs is inferred from identical serialized tool lists, not three separate token samples.
- With `n=20` and zero malformed calls, the Wilson 95% upper bound is 16.11%. This is expected at the precommitted minimum and is not a failure under the observed-rate rule.
- Daemon share uses two successful model-facing trial wall times.
- Standing-token probe USD was not retained. Excluded setup traces were not retained with all session IDs.

The excluded sessions were measurement setup, not members of the locked sample: one unclassifiable `max-turns=1` trace, one pre-sample invocation check, six attempts stopped for an empty-object classifier bug, and one classifier diagnostic. Three `CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR` MCP failures occurred before tool discovery and were not model malformed calls.
