## Required revision

Review 1 found four blocking design defects:

1. The claimed 80% power did not match the Holm-adjusted lower-bound pass rules or the available family count and budget.
2. ITT cost guardrails could punish successful arms and reward cheap failure.
3. The teaching-refusal population was undefined for Arm E and depended on a post-assignment event.
4. Four-family subsets could not support a one-sided 5% family-level sign-flip test.

The requested v3 package therefore:

- uses C-versus-B ITT success as the single Phase 1 confirmatory claim at one-sided alpha 0.05;
- treats direct, frontier, and teaching comparisons as component isolations that cannot create a headline pass;
- reports 62% expected power at a 15-point headline effect and a 19-point approximate 80% MDE against zero;
- commits 24 five-case families and at least eight families per class, with at least 12 in the frontier subset;
- uses a hierarchical bootstrap at 20 or more families and family-mean sign-flip permutation below 20;
- makes capability independent of cost and constrains efficiency-only to paired-success tokens plus dead-end and timeout/cap-hit guardrails;
- evaluates teaching recovery on all assigned recovery trials under intention to treat;
- makes pairing-complete budget stops, cap imbalance, Arm B completeness, ratio edge cases, schedule freezing, and Phase 2 freezing explicit.
